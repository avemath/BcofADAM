For years, thousands of people have followed Adam’s journey on our Facebook page, **Adam’s Village of Hope**. Your prayers, messages and support carried our family through the hardest days of our lives.

Now we’re building a home for everything we’ve learned, so that more families learn about drowning *before* it happens to them.

Here you’ll find Adam’s story, the layers of protection that keep young children safe around water, and resources for families living life after a nonfatal drowning.

Thank you for being part of Adam’s village.
