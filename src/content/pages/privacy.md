## What we collect

- **Messages you send us.** If you use our contact form or email us, we receive your name, email address and message. Our form is processed by a third-party form service (<span class="todo">name it, e.g. Formspree or Web3Forms</span>).
- **Donations.** Donations are processed by a third-party giving platform (<span class="todo">name it, e.g. Zeffy or Givebutter</span>). We receive your name, contact details and gift amount so we can thank you and send receipts. We never see or store your full card number.
- **Newsletter.** If you join our email list, we store your email address with our email provider. You can unsubscribe at any time.
- **Site statistics.** We may use privacy-friendly analytics that count visits without cookies or personal profiles.

The Water Watcher pledge on this site runs entirely in your browser. We don't collect your pledge answers.

## How we use it

We use your information only to reply to you, process and acknowledge donations, send updates you asked for, and understand how the site is used so we can improve it.

## What we never do

We never sell, rent or trade your personal information.

## Children's privacy

This website is intended for parents, caregivers and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Photos and stories

We only publish photos of children and families, and stories about them, with a parent or guardian's written permission. To ask us to remove something, contact us and we'll take care of it promptly.

## Contact

Questions about this policy? Please reach out through the Contact page, linked at the bottom of every page.
